# 📊 AI-Powered Finance Analytics & Responsible AI Labs

This repository contains a collection of hands-on Google Colab notebooks focused on:

* Credit Risk Analytics
* Fraud Detection
* Market Risk Analytics
* Explainable AI (XAI)
* Bias Detection & Fairness Metrics

* Transparency & AI Auditing

The notebooks are designed for learners, students, and professionals exploring the intersection of **Finance, Machine Learning, and Responsible AI**.

### Topics Covered

* Anomaly detection

* Precision vs Recall optimization
* Fraud analytics visualization
